Meet Dr. Samir Ouizi

Certified Canadian Wound Care Specialist

Originally from Casablanca, Morocco, Dr. Ouizi completed his Podiatric Medicine degree at the prestigious Institution Superieure De Podiatrie, Hopital Universitaire de Bruggman in Brussels, Belgium.


Dedicated to advancing podiatric medicine, he actively participates in international scientific research, ensuring his patients receive the most modern and effective treatments available without leaving their homes.

Key Credentials:
  • 25 Years of Clinical Experience
  • International Inter-professional Wound Care Certificate (Toronto, Ontario - 2006)
  • Specialist in Lower Limb Injury Prevention
